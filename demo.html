Defensive defaults

Try the following — each is a "thing the old <blink> never did":

  • Switch to another tab and back. The blinks were paused while hidden.
  • Scroll a blink off-screen. It stops until it returns to the viewport.
  • Enable reduced motion in your OS. Every blink stops and the text is shown fully, unless reduced-motion="ignore".
